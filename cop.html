Welcome to Nightingale

Nurturing Innovation, Integrity, and Academic Superiority

Established in the heart of Kupondole, Lalitpur, Nightingale International Secondary School has stood as a beacon of academic excellence for over three decades. We foster an environment where students acquire both intellectual rigor and personal values necessary for globally recognized achievement.

Our structured school blocks—from primary learning modules to dedicated secondary wings like Block-D—ensure personalized care, safety, and focused mentorship for every stage of student development.

Kindergarten & Montessori

Nurturing Early Curiosity & Play-Based Learning

Our Pre-Primary wing focuses on child-centered Montessori methodologies. We provide a safe, colorful, and engaging environment that stimulates sensory development, social skills, foundational literacy, and numeracy.

  • Interactive play-based curriculum
  • Safe, child-friendly indoor and outdoor play spaces
  • Dedicated & caring Montessori-trained teachers
Grade 1 to 6
